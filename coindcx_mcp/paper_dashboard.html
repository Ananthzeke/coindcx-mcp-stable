Simulation assumptions

Paper fills assume available liquidity at the best quote plus slippage. Contract rounding, market impact, exchange liquidation, and exact funding schedules are not reproduced. Stops can be missed between checks; stale or missing quotes prevent fills. During downtime positions stay open until the next valid quote.

Private model evaluation only. TimesFM 3.0 weights carry a non-commercial, non-production license. No exchange account keys are loaded by this agent. Past simulated results do not establish live performance.
